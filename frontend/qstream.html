FAQ

Questions we get asked a lot

Do I need to own a quantum computer? +
Not at all. Q-Stream provides a managed interface to our private quantum network via a simple API. You get all the benefits of quantum acceleration without owning, managing, or understanding any quantum hardware.
How is my data protected? +
We use CRYSTALS-Kyber post-quantum key exchange for all data in transit, AES-256 for data at rest, and our systems are HIPAA and SOC 2 Type II certified. Your computation inputs are never stored after task completion.
Which workloads benefit most from quantum offloading? +
Q-Stream delivers the greatest gains for NP-hard optimization problems: portfolio optimization, route planning, molecular simulation, hyperparameter tuning, and combinatorial search. Our SDK includes a task analyzer that tells you precisely which tasks in your codebase are quantum candidates.
How long does integration actually take? +
The SDK installs via npm or pip in 30 seconds. Initial configuration with your cloud provider takes 5–15 minutes using our guided CLI wizard. For enterprise on-prem deployments, our team handles setup within 48 hours with a dedicated solution engineer.
What happens if the quantum network is unavailable? +
Q-Stream automatically falls back to optimized classical execution with zero code changes required on your end. Our SLA guarantees continuous service availability. You'll see a flag in your dashboard when quantum routing was used vs. the classical fallback, so you always have full visibility.
Does Q-Stream support multi-cloud environments? +
Yes — Q-Stream has native connectors for AWS (Braket), Azure Quantum, Google Quantum AI, IBM Quantum, and IonQ. You can route tasks to any quantum backend and mix providers based on availability and cost using our intelligent scheduling layer.
Is there a free trial? What's included? +
Yes — all plans include a 14-day free trial with full Professional-tier access, including 10,000 quantum tasks, priority routing, and 24/7 support. No credit card required to start. At the end of the trial you can downgrade, upgrade, or cancel with one click.
How does billing work for quantum tasks? +
Each plan includes a monthly task allowance. A "quantum task" is counted as one optimization call to the quantum network, regardless of problem complexity. Unused tasks don't roll over. Overage is billed at $0.012 per task on Starter and $0.008 per task on Professional. Enterprise customers get custom volume pricing.
